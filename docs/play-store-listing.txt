📦 WC 2026 Stickers — Your Panini Collection Tracker

The ultimate companion app for collectors of the official FIFA World Cup 2026 Panini sticker album. Track every sticker across all 48 national teams and the exclusive FWC special section — 980 stickers in total.

🏆 BROWSE BY TEAM
Navigate teams organized by confederation (UEFA, CONMEBOL, CONCACAF, CAF, AFC, OFC). Each team shows a live progress bar so you always know how close you are to completing it. Tap any team to open its full sticker grid and manage quantities one by one.

⚡ QUICK ADD — LOG A WHOLE PACKET IN SECONDS
Received a new pack of stickers? Type the IDs all at once (e.g. ARG1 ENG5 FWC9 MAR7) and tap Add. Valid stickers appear as green chips, unknown codes appear in red so you never mistype. After adding, a session summary shows exactly what was new, what became a duplicate, and which teams got closer to completion.

❌ MISSING STICKERS
See the full list of stickers you still need, grouped by team. Share or copy the list to send to a friend before a trading session.

📋 DUPLICATES LIST
Every sticker you own more than one copy of is listed here with the exact count of extras. Use this during trades to know exactly what you can offer.

🤝 FRIEND MATCHER
Compare your duplicates with a friend's missing list to find the perfect matches. Maximise every trading session and complete your album faster.

🔍 SEARCH COLLECTION
Find any sticker instantly by sticker ID, team name, or player/card label. Filter, sort, and browse your entire collection in seconds.

📈 KPI RANKINGS
Curious which team you're closest to completing? Or which section has the most duplicates? The KPI screen breaks down your collection by confederation, completion %, and duplicate leaders.

📊 HOME DASHBOARD
At a glance: total collected, missing, duplicates, and your top favourite teams with progress bars. Your collection stats update in real time as you add stickers.

💾 100% OFFLINE — NO ACCOUNT NEEDED
All your data is stored locally on your device using a fast SQLite database. No internet connection required, no sign-up, no tracking, no ads.
