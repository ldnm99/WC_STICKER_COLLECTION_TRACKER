Privacy Policy

App: WC 2026 Stickers
Last updated: May 2026

1. Overview

WC 2026 Stickers is a 100% offline Android application. It does not collect, transmit, share, or store any personal data on external servers. All data you enter (sticker quantities, preferences) is stored exclusively on your own device.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no data whatsoever. Specifically:

  • No names, email addresses, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No usage analytics or crash reports
  • No financial or payment information

3. Data Storage

The app stores your sticker collection data in a local SQLite database on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not backed up to any cloud service by this app. Standard Android backup (if enabled by you) may back up this data to your Google account under your own Google privacy settings.

4. Internet Access

WC 2026 Stickers does not require or request internet permission. The app functions entirely offline.

5. Third-Party Services

This app does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or social media platforms.

6. Children's Privacy

This app does not collect any data from users of any age, including children under 13. It is safe for use by all age groups.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we update this privacy policy, we will revise the "Last updated" date above. Since no data is collected, no material changes affecting user privacy are anticipated.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, you can contact the developer through the Google Play Store listing page for WC 2026 Stickers.
